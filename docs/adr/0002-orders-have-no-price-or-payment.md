# Orders are booking requests, with no price or payment

The original brief asked for "products" and "orders", which reads like an online shop. For Deutsch mit Asmaa a product is a course and an order is a request to book one: it has no price and takes no payment, because prices and times are only agreed with Frau Asmaa over WhatsApp (`info.md`), and she has not approved publishing them. Adding prices or online payment later means new approval from her, a payment provider, and changes to the order model.
